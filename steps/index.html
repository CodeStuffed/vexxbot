Stacker Robot Build

Version A, step by step. Coloured parts are new in this step, grey parts are already on the robot.

Students build it. This guide shows one way to put the robot together. Under the RECF Student-Centered Policy the team does the building, wiring and coding, and should be able to explain every part. Change anything you can make better. A grown-up or older student does the cutting and drilling; small screws and nuts are a choking risk for little kids.
  • T15 star driver, for the screws
  • 11/32 in wrench, for the nylock nuts
  • 5/64 in hex key, for shaft collar screws
  • Hacksaw and file, to cut channel and shafts
  • Drill with an 11/64 in bit, for the drilled holes
  • Vise, to bend the aligner strips
