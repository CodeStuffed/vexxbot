How to build the stacker robot

A tall lift robot that starts 16.7 in tall and stretches to 46 in. This site has the step-by-step guide, every part with counts, the cut list and short videos of the robot.

Before you start

Students build it. Under the RECF Student-Centered Policy the team does the building, wiring and coding, and should be able to explain every part. Use these steps to learn how it goes together, then change it, test it and make it yours. Don't copy this site's pictures or tables into an engineering notebook.

Measure first. This robot has only been built in the computer. Every hole and screw was checked by the model, but before step 1, measure one C-channel: the side walls should be 0.5 in tall and their holes 0.25 in from the outside face. If yours is different, the spacer sizes will change.

An adult does the cutting and drilling. Wear safety glasses. Small screws and nuts are a choking risk for little kids.

Tools

  • T15 star driver, for the screws
  • 11/32 in wrench, for the nylock nuts
  • 5/64 in hex key, for shaft collar screws
  • Hacksaw and file, to cut channel and shafts
  • Drill with an 11/64 in bit
  • Vise, to bend the aligner strips

These tool sizes are from memory, so check them against your kit.

How to read the steps

  • Coloured parts are new in that step. Grey parts are already on the robot.
  • Hole numbers count from one end. Rails: hole 1 is at the back. Lift channels: hole 1 is at the bottom. Cross channels: hole 1 is on the left, standing behind the robot.
  • A C-channel has a flat middle (the web) with 2 or 3 rows of holes, and two side walls (the flanges).
  • Nylock is the nut with the blue plastic ring. Every screw gets one unless it screws into a motor or a standoff. For anything that spins, stop tightening while it still turns freely.
  • Shaft collar is the small ring with a set screw. Tighten it last, once the shaft turns freely.
  • Screws are named by length in inches. All are #8-32 star drive.

How the lift works

The lift is a cascade: three C-channel stages that slide inside each other, like a fire-truck ladder. The winch winds in 10 in of string and the top stage rises 20 in, because a second string, which never changes length, rides over a pulley on the middle stage.

Version B

The claw hanger plates are 3.5 in longer so the claw reaches the floor. Leave off the three bent funnel strips: the claw motor sits where they would be. Keep the flat strip on each rail. Version B's parts are in the parts list.

Not in the model

Each drive wheel is screwed to its 48T gear so they turn as one, on round plastic inserts on the 2.5 in screw. Also not drawn: chain, string, wires, and the Brain, battery and radio mounts. They are listed under "Also needed" in the parts list.

Before you drive

  • The lift and arm move by hand through their whole travel with nothing catching.
  • Every nylock is on. Every collar is tight. Every shaft turns freely.
  • The robot fits in an 18 x 18 x 18 in box with the lift down and the arm folded back.
